# Meal Planner — Requirements

## 1. Overview

Meal Planner helps users organize recipes, find meals based on available ingredients, and plan their daily meals.

## 2. MVP Requirements

### Recipe Management
- **R1:** Create and save recipes with names and ingredients.
- **R2:** View saved recipes and their ingredients.
- **R3:** Edit existing recipes and save changes.
- **R4:** Delete a recipe that is no longer needed.

### Recipe Discovery
- **R4:** Search for recipes by name or keyword.
- **R5:** Find recipes based on available ingredients.

### Daily Meal Planning
- **R6:** Assign recipes to breakfast, lunch, or dinner for the current day.
- **R7:** View assigned meals and their associated recipes.

## 3. Future Requirements

- **Recipe Organization:** Tags, categories, dietary filters, and serving adjustments.
- **Pantry Management:** Track and update available ingredients.
- **Grocery Planning:** Create and manage shopping lists.
- **Meal Calendar:** Schedule and manage meals across multiple days.
- **Account Preferences:** Manage profiles, dietary restrictions, allergies, and serving preferences.

## 4. Walking Skeleton Scope

The current implementation supports creating, viewing, searching by name, and deleting recipes, with persistent data storage. Remaining MVP features will be developed in subsequent milestones.
